Academic Journey

From an undergraduate grounding in Indian polity to doctoral research on gender and governance — three stages, one continuous line of inquiry.

Timeline

Education

2019 – 2022

B.A. (Hons.) Political Science

Kamala Nehru College, University of Delhi

Developed a foundational grounding in Indian polity, political theory, and foreign policy. Went on to complete a research mentorship with the RISE Cell during the final year, producing a paper on cyberstalking law in India.

2022 – 2024

Master of Public Policy (MPP)

Indian Institute of Technology Tirupati

Dissertation investigated women as policymakers in Vadodara, Gujarat — an empirical study that became the intellectual seedbed for current doctoral work, and the point at which methodological questions about studying gender and governance first took shape.

2024 – Present

Ph.D., Humanities and Social Sciences

Indian Institute of Technology Tirupati

Doctoral research examines the political conditions and institutional structures shaping women's participation in formal governance in India, with growing emphasis on computational (NLP-based) analysis of political text and discourse.
